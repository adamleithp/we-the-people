# We The People — Project Overview

> Conceptual source for the movement. Read this before any content, copy, or product work.
> Derived from the founding documents in [`docs/`](./docs).

## What it is

**We The People (WTP)** is a UK political movement. Mission: **"Reunite the Country, Transform the System."** Launching ~2026, aiming at the 2029 general election. Website: `wethepeoplegather.com`.

Core mechanism = **Citizens' Assemblies**, rebranded **"Councils of the People" (CPs)**: ~100 randomly-selected, demographically representative ordinary people hear all sides of a divisive issue, deliberate, and forge compromise policy. "Jury service applied to politics."

## Mission & sentiment

- **Mission:** heal national division, reclaim power for the people, rebirth democracy for the 21st century. Immigration tackled first; everything else follows.
- **Sentiment:** **hope after a dark night of division.** Unity over tribalism — "We Are Many, We Are One." Inclusive: *everyone* welcome, all political sides. Non-partisan, anti-extremist, anti-ego. Emotional, almost spiritual ("hidden unity," ancient wisdom + quantum physics). Bold and revolutionary but warm — never alienating.
- **Tagline:** **"BE PART OF HOPE."**

## The argument (10 steps)

**Part A — The Theory**
1. **Beyond politicians** — swapping parties won't fix it; the system rests on "us vs them."
2. **Power back to the people** — democracy = rule by people; tap the "Collective Intelligence" of 70M.
3. **Heal division** — peace needs compromise, empathy, listening.
4. **Citizens' Assemblies** — proven (Ireland abortion, France assisted dying, UK climate); 700+ held worldwide.

**Part B — In Practice**
5. **First CP: Immigration** — start with the hardest issue. National, independent, Truth & Reconciliation spirit; a 10–14 day event with music, food, celebrities, livestream, app, documentary.
6. **The Movement** — nationwide banner for peace + unity; decentralised "franchise"; wildcat local Councils ("We The People of Orkney, Dover, Brixton…").
7. **People's Manifesto** — series of CPs on divisive issues → policy written by the people; pressure politicians by 2029.
8. **Deep system change** — if WTP becomes a party and wins: install CPs at every level of government, write the first UK written constitution, hybrid model (politicians become implementers). Then **the party disbands** — "take power to give it away." ~10yr / 2 terms.
9. **Roadmap** — Phase 1 prep (2026) → build-up → first CP (Autumn 2027) → 2029 election.
10. **How to help** — sign up, volunteer skills.

## Core Principles (10)

First principle: **put humanity, collectively, first** — above any party, policy or group. Then: Everyone Welcome · Come As You Are · Do Unto Others · Curious & Open-Minded · Both/And (beyond binary) · Forgiveness · Democracy as a Way of Life · Human Over Machine · Our Hidden Unity. The principles are "open source" — adaptable.

## The Vow

Members pledge to hold personal political loyalties **secondary** to WTP's objectives (reunite the country, transform the system) until achieved, and to uphold the principles in daily life — or may be asked to leave any active role.

## Brand & website direction

- **Primary colour:** British Racing Green `#004225` — British, earthy, non-divisive. Dawn yellows as secondary.
- **Logo:** WE THE PEOPLE in caps, black/white.
- **Imagery:** people's faces + the British land (mountains, rivers, meadows); avoid any identity-politics slant.
- **Site goals:** explain the steps, answer FAQs, and above all **gather sign-ups** (primary CTA: "Join Us"). Audience = "literally everyone."
- **Tone:** effortlessly cool but simple; bold action, journey toward unity; works seamlessly on mobile + desktop.
- **Likely partner:** ISWE Foundation (runs assemblies).
