# Story photographs — PLACEHOLDERS, NOT LICENSED

Every image in this folder was pulled out of the client's copy doc
(`docs/Copy update 28 Sept.docx`), where they were pasted as mood references.
Most are press or deck photos the movement does not own. The doc itself says
several are "hard to license" (the deck photos, Stormzy at Glastonbury).

**Do not ship these publicly.** Before the site goes public, replace each file
with a licensed original of the same name (any size or format: Astro resizes
it to WebP at build), or swap in a different licensed image.

The pasted copies are also small (~735px wide), so they look soft full-bleed.
Licensed originals should be at least 2400px wide.

**Done so far:** all of `/problem` and `/hope`, plus the replaced `/be-part-of-it` slots, hold full-size Unsplash photos
(Unsplash licence, credit not required but given below).
